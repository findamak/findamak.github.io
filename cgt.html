What this is modelling

The comparison is intentionally simple: the old rules apply the 50% CGT discount to the whole nominal gain. The new rules keep the discount for the pre-1 July 2027 portion and apply CPI indexation to the post-1 July 2027 portion, with a 30% minimum tax rate on that post-transition real gain. For assets owned before 1 July 2027, investors can compare time apportionment against an optional market valuation split.

Old / discount model: taxable gain equals the nominal capital gain after a 50% discount if the asset was held for more than 12 months; otherwise the full nominal gain is taxed.

New / time-apportionment CPI model: the nominal gain is apportioned by holding days before and after 1 July 2027. The pre-transition portion receives the old 50% discount. The post-transition portion is reduced by CPI for the post-transition holding period and then taxed at the higher of your marginal rate and 30%.

New / market valuation CPI model: if you enter a 1 July 2027 asset value, the pre-transition gain is calculated from cost base to that market value, and the post-transition gain is calculated from that market value to sale proceeds.
